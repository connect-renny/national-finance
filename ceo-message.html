CEO Message

Five Years of Leadership, and Achievements Made by an Exceptional Team

Five years of challenges, achievements, and working alongside a strong and dedicated team. Since joining National Finance, the journey has been filled with milestones and experiences that have shaped our path and strengthened my belief that true achievement is never the result of individual effort alone, but of a team united by a shared vision, a sense of responsibility, and a common ambition.

Together we have grown our reach, strengthened our services and stayed close to the customers and communities we serve. Each milestone belongs to every colleague who gave their time, ideas and commitment along the way.

As we look ahead, I am confident that the same spirit will carry us further. Thank you for your trust, your dedication and the pride you bring to National Finance every day.

Tariq Sulaiman Al Farsi Chief Executive Officer
